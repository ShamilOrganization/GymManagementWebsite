Delete your Gymio account

How to request deletion of your account and your data

App: Gymio (com.shamil.gymio)
Developer: Shamil · shamilpp4115@gmail.com

How to request deletion

Choose either method. Both reach the same place.

From inside the app

  1. Open Gymio and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Contact & feedback.
  4. Send the message: “Please delete my account”.

By email

  1. Email shamilpp4115@gmail.com
  2. Subject: Delete my Gymio account
  3. Include the phone number registered on your account, so we can identify it.
We verify the request before deleting anything. We confirm it comes from the account holder — otherwise anyone could delete someone else’s account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

Data What happens
Name, phone number, password Deleted
Profile photo Deleted, including the image file itself
Progress photos Deleted, including the image files themselves
Attendance / check-in history Deleted
Membership payment records held in Gymio Deleted
Messages you sent us via Contact & feedback Deleted
Sign-in sessions Deleted — you are signed out everywhere

What your gym may keep

Your gym is a separate business that uses Gymio to run its own operations. If your gym keeps its own records of your membership — for example in its accounts, for tax or legal reasons — those records belong to the gym, not to Gymio, and deleting your Gymio account does not remove them. Contact your gym directly about anything it holds.

How long it takes

We action deletion requests as soon as we can verify them, and complete them within 30 days at the latest. There is no additional retention period on our side: once deleted, the data above is gone and cannot be recovered.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual progress photos yourself in the app, from your timeline. To remove other specific data while keeping your account, ask us using either method above and say what you want removed.

You can also edit your name and phone number at any time from your profile.
