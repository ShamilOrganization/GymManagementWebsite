Account and Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete your Gymio account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

  • From the app: go to Settings → Contact & feedback and send us a message asking for your account to be deleted, or
  • By email: write to shamilpp4115@gmail.com from the phone number on the account, with the subject “Delete my Gymio account”.

We verify that the request comes from the account holder, then delete your account, profile details, photos, attendance history and payment records. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Your gym may retain its own separate records of your membership where it is required to do so by law (for example, for accounting or tax purposes); those records are held by your gym, not by Gymio.
